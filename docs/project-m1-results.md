## Prompt 3: Growth

**Prompt:** Suggest 3 low-cost AI tools a bakey in lahore can useto attract umiversity sudents with a budget of 100000 PKR per month.
**AI answer:** With a budget of **100,000 PKR per month**, you are actually in a very strong position. Most AI tools are either free or cost a small 
monthly subscription (usually $10–$30). This means you can use AI to handle the creative work and spend the bulk of your budget on 
**targeted Meta Ads (Instagram/Facebook)** to actually reach the students.

Here are 3 low-cost AI tools specifically suited for a bakery targeting university students:

### 1. Canva (Magic Studio)
**Best for: "Aesthetic" Visuals & Story Design**
University students are driven by aesthetics. If your food looks "Instagrammable," they will come. Canva’s new AI features (Magic Studio) 
allow you to create professional designs without being a graphic designer.
*   **How to use it:** Use **"Magic Edit"** to change the background of a cake photo to something trendy, or **"Text-to-Image"** to 
create surreal, eye-catching backgrounds for your announcements.
*   **Student Attraction:** Create "Study Break" vouchers or "Exam Survival Kits" graphics that look modern and clean.
*   **Cost:** Free version available; Pro is roughly **2,000–4,000 PKR/month**.

### 2. CapCut (AI Video Editor)
**Best for: Viral TikToks and Instagram Reels**
Students spend more time on Reels and TikTok than anywhere else. CapCut is owned by ByteDance (TikTok) and has incredible AI integration.
*   **How to use it:** Use the **"Auto-Caption"** feature (essential because many students watch videos on mute in class) and **"AI 
Templates"** to sync your bakery clips to trending music automatically. You can even use the **"Script-to-Video"** tool to turn a simple 
idea (e.g., "Top 3 brownies for late-night studying") into a rough video draft.
*   **Student Attraction:** Post "ASMR" baking videos or "Behind the Scenes" clips of fresh cookies coming out of the oven.
*   **Cost:** Free (Pro version is very affordable).

### 3. ChatGPT (Plus/GPT-4)
**Best for: Relatable Copywriting & Marketing Strategy**
Writing for Gen Z requires a specific tone—it needs to be witty, casual, and not "try-hard." ChatGPT can help you brainstorm these 
angles.
*   **How to use it:** Don't just ask it to "write a post." Tell it: *"You are a trendy social media manager in Lahore. Write 5 
funny Instagram captions for a brownie delivery targeting students at LUMS or FAST who are stressed about finals. Use a mix of English 
and Urdu (Roman Urdu)."*
*   **Student Attraction:** Create a "Student Loyalty Program" or brainstorm a "Campus Brand Ambassador" strategy to get students to 
promote you in exchange for free treats.
*   **Cost:** Free version is great; Plus version is ~$20 (**approx. 5,600 PKR/month**).
