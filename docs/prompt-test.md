With a budget of **100,000 PKR per month**, you cannot afford to build custom AI from scratch, but 
you have a very healthy budget to implement **SaaS (Software as a Service) AI tools**. 

Late deliveries in bakeries usually happen due to three reasons: **inefficient routing, poor demand 
forecasting (overwhelmed staff), and communication gaps.**

Here are three AI-driven solutions tailored to your budget and problem:

---

### 1. AI-Powered Route Optimization (The "Last-Mile" Solution)
**The Problem:** Drivers taking inefficient routes, getting stuck in traffic, or sequencing 
deliveries poorly.

**The Solution:** Implement an AI routing tool like **Circuit for Teams** or **OptimoRoute**. 
These tools use AI algorithms to analyze thousands of possible route combinations in seconds. 
Instead of a driver deciding where to go, the AI provides the absolute fastest sequence of stops 
based on real-time traffic, distance, and delivery windows.

*   **How it helps:** It reduces mileage and ensures drivers spend less time on the road and more 
time delivering. It also provides "Estimated Time of Arrival" (ETA) updates to the bakery manager.
*   **Estimated Cost:** 15,000 – 30,000 PKR / month (depending on the number of drivers).
*   **Tool Suggestion:** *Circuit for Teams* or *Route4Me*.

### 2. Predictive Demand Forecasting (The "Preparation" Solution)
**The Problem:** The bakery is caught off guard by a surge in orders (e.g., Friday rushes or 
holiday spikes), leading to a bottleneck in the kitchen and delays in dispatch.

**The Solution:** Use a lightweight AI forecasting tool or an AI-integrated POS (Point of Sale) 
system like **Square** or **Loyverse** (with AI plugins). 
These tools analyze historical sales data to predict exactly how many cakes/pastries you will 
likely sell on a specific day and hour.

*   **How it helps:** If the AI predicts a 30% increase in orders for next Sunday, you can schedule 
extra delivery riders and pre-bake items. Late deliveries are often a *production* problem, not 
just a *driving* problem.
*   **Estimated Cost:** 10,000 – 20,000 PKR / month.
*   **Tool Suggestion:** *Forecastly* or integrating *Google Sheets with AI (Coefficient.io)* to 
predict trends.

### 3. AI-Automated Customer Communication (The "Transparency" Solution)
**The Problem:** Manual updating of customers ("Your order is on the way") takes up staff time and 
leads to "Where is my order?" calls that distract the dispatch team.

**The Solution:** Deploy an AI-driven WhatsApp Bot via **Interakt** or **Gallabox** (which use the 
WhatsApp Business API). 
You can set up an AI agent that automatically triggers notifications when a driver marks an order 
as "Out for Delivery." It can also handle customer queries about delivery status using AI, without 
a human needing to type a response.

*   **How it helps:** It removes the administrative burden from the bakery staff, allowing them to 
focus on getting orders out the door faster. It also manages customer expectations, reducing the 
"perceived" lateness.
*   **Estimated Cost:** 20,000 – 35,000 PKR / month (includes API costs).
*   **Tool Suggestion:** *Interakt.shop* or *ManyChat* for WhatsApp.
